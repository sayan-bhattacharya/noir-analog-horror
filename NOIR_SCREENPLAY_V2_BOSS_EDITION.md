**Mythology of Nistobdhopur:** A real Bengal village whose geography is gradually changing. Initially it seems ordinary; then maps, weather reports, and physical reality begin contradicting each other.

---

## 1. THREE VISUAL LAYERS & ANTI-MONOTONY RULES
Do **not** apply a uniform VHS filter over the entire film. Separate the image into three distinct visual languages:
1. **Broadcast Graphics (Institutional Authenticity):** Clean, functional 1994 typography, cartographic maps, coordinates, and station IDs.
2. **Environmental Footage (Physical Reality):** Atmospheric, cinematic, humid Bengal landscapes (cleanly photographed, zero forced glitches).
3. **Surveillance Imagery (The Hidden Observer):** Locked-off frames, camera labels, and cold telemetry.

*Horror emerges from the **contradiction** between clean broadcast graphics and the physical world (e.g., a map marks an extra location the camera cannot find).*

### Three Risks Actively Avoided
1. **No CRT Glitch Overuse:** Preserve clean, stable frames when the audience should feel deeply uncomfortable.
2. **Do Not Overexplain Trinetra:** Let its circular geometry recur across maps, shadows, and mandalas long before naming it.
3. **Keep the Businessman Grounded:** No monster makeup; an ordinary man in a dark suit behaving in a way that is almost, but not quite, physically possible.

---

## 2. THE FIVE-CHANNEL ARCHITECTURE (SHARED SIGNATURE: THE CRIMSON POINT)
Let shots breathe. Never rapid-cut channels without reason. Switch channels **only when information is transferred, contradicted, or revealed**:
- **`CH 03` — Geography & Weather (Reality):** Villages, maps, rainfall, rivers, topography.
- **`CH 07` — Domestic Observation (Subject):** Bimal's bedroom, his breathing, his sleep.
- **`CH 09` — Emergency Information (Rules):** Unexplained government-style warnings, coordinates, rules.
- **`CH 11` — Educational Programming (Foreshadowing):** *Sahaj Path* / Nandalal Bose style linocut diagrams explaining impossible things innocently.
- **`BTN 24` — News (Indifference):** Reports impossible events as ordinary regional news.

*Hidden Continuity Signature across all 5 channels (and Berlin):* **The Crimson Point** (a single small red LED / dot / cursor).
