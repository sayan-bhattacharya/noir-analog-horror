### Broadcast Lore Context
In the early hours of May 14, 1994, terrestrial transmitters across southern Bengal ceased regular programming (a scheduled telecast of a late-night classical Sarod recital). Instead, television sets equipped with VHF receivers flickered violently, locked onto channel VHF 04, and broadcast an uncatalogued Emergency Advisory from the West Bengal Disaster Management Authority (পশ্চিমবঙ্গ বিপর্যয় মোকাবিলা দফতর). 

No official records exist in the Prasar Bharati archives acknowledging this broadcast. The surviving footage originates from an off-air VHS recording captured on a National Panasonic NV-G10 VCR in rural Burdwan.

## 🏛️ 3. THE FOUR THEMATIC PILLARS

### PILLAR I: 1990s Doordarshan CRT Interruption & Bureaucratic Horror
- **Visual Vocabulary**: Corrupted Doordarshan spiral eye emblem, blinking `REC ●` glyph, amber and phosphor-green OSD typography, severe horizontal barrel distortion, chromatic aberration, tape head-switching noise.
- **Dread Catalyst**: Institutional breakdown. A government broadcaster calmly admitting that standard reality has collapsed and instructing citizens to surrender to biological transformation.

### PILLAR II: Courage in Rural Bengal (*Ekla Bari* in Nistobdhopur)
- **Visual Vocabulary**: The solitary, crooked ancestral two-storey brick-and-mud house (*ekla bari*) surrounded by a vast, pitch-black flat expanse of waterlogged paddy fields. Wind-tattered broad leaves of wild banana plants (*kola gachh*), tall creaking betel-nut palms (*supari gachh*), and a rusted cast-iron tubewell (*chapa-kol*) dripping phantom water into crimson mud.
- **Dread Catalyst**: Rural claustrophobia within infinite desolation. No neighbors, no electricity, only the humid monsoon wind rustling wet foliage.

### PILLAR III: Kafka in Bengal (বিমলবাবুর রূপান্তর — The Metamorphosis of Bimal Babu)
- **Visual Vocabulary**: A stifling ancestral bedroom. High ceiling, peeling lime plaster, antique wooden bed (*segun kaather khat*), shrouded beneath a drooping, yellowish mosquito net (*moshari*). Cold polished red-oxide floor (*laal mejhe*).
- **Transformation**: Bimal Babu, an unremarkable clerk, wakes to find his ribcage splitting open. Jointed cockroach and scorpion limbs (*telachoka o bichha*) sprout in jerky stop-motion jitter. He falls from the bed; his claws click against the red-oxide stone.
- **Dread Catalyst**: Biological alienation. The human body betrayed by ancient subterranean biology.

### PILLAR IV: The Uncanny Animal & Tantric Cosmic Dread
- **Visual Vocabulary**: A large, gaunt black tomcat (*kalo hulo beRaal*) perched motionless on a rustic split-bamboo fence (*baasher beRaal*). Its neck stretches dozens of feet vertically into the night sky. In the bruised purple clouds above, the sky splits open like a cosmic eyelid: the *Trinetra* (ত্রিনেত্র / Third Eye), a bloodshot celestial pupil observing the ritual.
- **Dread Catalyst**: Folk superstition (the cat as the vahana of Goddess Shasthi, harbinger of children's doom) elevating into cosmic cosmic insignificance.
