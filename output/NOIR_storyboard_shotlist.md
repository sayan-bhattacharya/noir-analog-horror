# NOIR — The Nishidak Broadcast — Storyboard shot list

The shot prompts are designed for local image generation in Imagination Studio or another image tool. Existing stills are visual references, not locked frames.

## 01 · The mark in the glass (00:00–01:30)
**Act:** Prologue  
**Frame:** Wide, locked, centered  
**Camera:** Hold; the droplet trembles once and falls  
**Story:** A nearly black CRT. A thin amber ring and one suspended black drop are just visible. The first ripple disappears before the audience can decide what it means.  
**Text:** MAY 14, 1994 · DO NOT ANSWER A FAMILIAR VOICE AFTER MIDNIGHT.  
**Sound:** Low electrical presence; let the silence around it stay audible.  
**Image prompt:**

A restrained 1990s CRT close-wide frame, almost black. One fine amber circular aperture above a single glossy black droplet and a delicate water ripple. Symmetrical, minimal, analog texture, subtle scanlines. Hold the image in darkness; no words or logos. 4:3 picture inside a black 16:9 matte.

## 02 · The unlisted transmission (01:00–01:30)
**Act:** Act I · The call  
**Frame:** Centered graphic insert  
**Camera:** Slow signal lock; one brief tracking slip  
**Story:** A calm station slate resolves out of snow. Identification arrives first; the warning follows after a beat.  
**Text:** VHF 04 / DD-1 BANGLA · MAY 14, 1994  
**Sound:** Soft carrier hum; no stinger on the warning.  
**Image prompt:**

A sober 1994 Bengali public television emergency broadcast slate seen through a curved CRT, dark navy-black glass, a small phosphor amber spiral symbol, restrained green station data, one narrow muted-red alert band. Leave generous empty space for legible type to be added in post. No invented garbled lettering, no contemporary interface.

## 03 · The house listens back (01:30–03:15)
**Act:** Act I · The call  
**Frame:** Extreme wide; house small in frame  
**Camera:** Almost imperceptible push-in  
**Story:** A lonely two-storey Bengal house in flat wet fields. One shutter is slightly open. A single banana leaf turns against the wind.  
**Text:** VHF 04 / DD-1 BANGLA · SECTOR: NISTOBDHOPUR  
**Sound:** Monsoon wind held low beneath the drone.  
**Image prompt:**

Wide 4:3 archival frame of a solitary two-storey rural Bengal house in waterlogged fields at night, banana plants and distant palms, damp plaster, one upstairs shutter open a few inches. Keep the house small in a large empty landscape. Restrained near-monochrome, faint warm window light, 1990s CRT grain; no text.

## 04 · Bimal chooses to go (03:15–06:00)
**Act:** Act I · The call  
**Frame:** Medium-wide interior, profile  
**Camera:** Static; netting drifts after he passes  
**Story:** Bimal sits listening. He checks the dark doorway, puts on his shoes, pauses at the threshold, then steps outside. His decision is small and deliberate.  
**Text:** RULE 01 · DO NOT FOLLOW A VOICE YOU CANNOT PLACE.  
**Sound:** A faint tonal shift suggests a familiar call; no clear voice.  
**Image prompt:**

A quiet, human-scale bedroom in an old rural Bengal house at night. Bimal Babu, an ordinary tired clerk in rumpled cotton work clothes, sits at the edge of a wooden bed beneath a sheer mosquito net, listening toward a dark open window. Profile medium-wide composition; he is alert but not terrified. Damp lime plaster, kerosene-lamp warmth, subtle 1990s film grain, no supernatural creature, no text.

## 05 · The pump makes a pool (06:00–09:15)
**Act:** Act II · The conduit  
**Frame:** Low medium shot, foreground pump  
**Camera:** Slow push toward the dark surface  
**Story:** The handpump moves once by itself. Black liquid spreads where water should soak in. Bimal marks the edge with a stone; the stone later appears beneath the surface.  
**Text:** No new card. Let the image hold.  
**Sound:** The drone thins into an uneasy near-silence.  
**Image prompt:**

Low, patient 4:3 frame of an old rusted cast-iron handpump in a Bengal courtyard after rain. A small amount of glossy black liquid gathers beneath the spout and forms a clean circular pool in red earth. The pump is still; no gore or fantasy glow. Dark banana leaves, distant house, restrained highlights, 1990s analog film texture.

## 06 · A cat, then a tower (10:00–12:30)
**Act:** Act III · The witness  
**Frame:** Long lens; fence across lower third  
**Camera:** Locked; the neck extends in two slow stages  
**Story:** A small black cat sits deadpan on the bamboo rail. Give the ordinary silhouette time to register. Its neck then rises beyond the frame while its body remains still.  
**Text:** No explanatory warning.  
**Sound:** The score gains weight without accelerating the cut.  
**Image prompt:**

A gaunt black rural tomcat sits motionless on a split-bamboo fence at night, small against a Bengal courtyard and a low monsoon sky. Keep the animal almost ordinary and faintly absurd. Long-lens 4:3 composition with negative space above for a later neck-extension effect; monochrome CRT archive texture, no giant eye yet, no text.

## 07 · The sky looks down (12:30–14:00)
**Act:** Act III · The witness  
**Frame:** Low angle, cat and sky share axis  
**Camera:** Slow tilt up; stop when the eye meets lens  
**Story:** The cloud cover opens around a colossal eye. It does not hunt; it simply settles its gaze on Bimal. The prologue's circle returns in a new scale.  
**Text:** Let the visual rhyme carry the beat.  
**Sound:** One broad swell; no impact hit.  
**Image prompt:**

A colossal, still eye emerging slowly from bruised monsoon clouds above a Bengal bamboo fence. A long-necked black cat anchors the eye to the ground. Near-monochrome storm palette with one restrained crimson iris and small sulfur-yellow cat eyes. Cosmic scale but composed and quiet, 4:3 archival frame, no lettering.

## 08 · The courtyard tips away (14:00–18:00)
**Act:** Act IV · The fall  
**Frame:** High angle into a circular void  
**Camera:** Bimal falls backward; camera remains with him  
**Story:** A crack reaches Bimal's shoe. The earth tilts with terrible calm. He reaches for the fence and misses. The pool becomes a dark opening; the world drains to one pale point.  
**Text:** No warning card during the fall.  
**Sound:** Follow the score's natural drop; avoid added impacts.  
**Image prompt:**

A small human silhouette falling backward into a vast black circular abyss, fragments of a Bengal courtyard and a few horizontal CRT scanlines receding above him. Preserve clear negative space and a strong 4:3 composition. Slow, weightless, restrained monochrome, no spiral tunnel, no extra characters, no text.

## 09 · A clean, quiet place (18:00–21:00)
**Act:** Act V · The corridor  
**Frame:** Centered deep one-point perspective  
**Camera:** Slow dolly forward behind Bimal  
**Story:** Bimal stands in a spotless fluorescent corridor. He checks his hands and clothes, then walks toward the EXIT 8 sign. The yellow tactile strip is the only color.  
**Text:** EXIT 8 — environmental sign only.  
**Sound:** Near-silence; steady fluorescent hum.  
**Image prompt:**

A sterile, empty underground corridor with white square tiles, cool fluorescent ceiling lights, a single yellow tactile strip, deep one-point perspective. Bimal Babu, seen from behind in rumpled work clothes, stands small in the frame. Near-monochrome, crisp and quiet, no other person, no glitch, no extra signage beyond EXIT 8.

## 10 · The man between the lights (21:00–29:40)
**Act:** Act V · The corridor  
**Frame:** Long shot, centered figure; then eye detail  
**Camera:** Locked; approach advances only between flickers  
**Story:** A suited man stands on the strip with an empty briefcase. Bimal tests the flicker pattern, learns the corridor's rhythm, then sees the broadcast spiral reflected in the man's eye.  
**Text:** IF YOU NOTICE A CHANGE, TURN BACK. / IF NOTHING HAS CHANGED, KEEP WALKING.  
**Sound:** The fluorescent hum barely shifts.  
**Image prompt:**

A pale, ordinary middle-aged man in a dark grey suit stands motionless on the yellow tactile strip of a clean white-tiled underground corridor, holding a closed briefcase. Symmetrical long shot, fluorescent stillness, no horror makeup. A subtle CRT spiral reflection in one eye for a later close-up. Restrained, clinical, no text.

## 11 · The circle without the drop (29:40–30:22)
**Act:** Coda  
**Frame:** Centered graphic insert  
**Camera:** Static hold; then cut to black  
**Story:** The red playhead reaches the end. The amber mark from the opening returns alone. This time there is no droplet beneath it.  
**Text:** No title card. End on black.  
**Sound:** Resolve toward silence; hold four seconds.  
**Image prompt:**

The same nearly black CRT composition as the opening: a thin amber circular mark centered in deep black. No droplet, no ripple, no text. Fine scanlines and faint glass curvature. Hold still and let the image fade to absolute black.
